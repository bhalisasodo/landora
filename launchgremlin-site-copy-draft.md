## 2. The Leak

**Section header:**
The Leak

**Body (default/general):**
Somewhere between your Instagram bio and your booking calendar, people disappear. Not because they weren't interested — because your website made them work for it.

A menu with no "Book Now." A contact form nobody checks. A homepage that looks lovely and asks for nothing.

That's not a design problem. That's revenue walking out the door.

**Per-industry variant lines (swap into "The Leak" depending on active demo):**
- **Spa & Wellness:** "They wanted to book a massage. They got a PDF price list and a WhatsApp number they had to copy by hand."
- **Restaurant & Cafe:** "They wanted a table for two, tonight. Your site sent them to a menu PDF and a phone number — during dinner service."
- **Fitness Studio:** "They were ready to book a trial class. Your site asked them to 'enquire' and wait for a callback."

**General leak archetypes (reference — underlying problems the variant lines above dramatize):**
1. The Dead End — homepage looks good, no clear next action.
2. The Scavenger Hunt — booking/contact buried multiple clicks deep.
3. The Ghost Form — contact form emails an inbox nobody checks promptly.
4. The Mismatch — ad/social traffic lands on a homepage that doesn't match the promise.
5. The Desktop Trap — site "works" on mobile but wasn't designed for it.
