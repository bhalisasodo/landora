# Landora — Brand Identity Pack (for logo & icon generation)

## 1. What Landora Is
Landora is a specialized landing-page service under the LaunchGremlin umbrella. It exists to fix a single problem: leaking sales pipelines caused by weak landing pages. Landora builds high-conversion landing pages that turn lookers into bookers.

**Tagline reference (from parent brand, adaptable):** "One link that turns lookers into bookers."
**Supporting line reference:** "You don't need a prettier website. You need one that closes."

## 2. Brand Positioning
- Premium, elegant, quietly confident — not playful or mascot-driven.
- Landora is the polished, product-like face of the service: think "a tool," not "an agency personality."
- Reference quality bar: Apple's online store — restrained, high-contrast, generous whitespace, nothing decorative that isn't earning its place.
- Landora is a sub-brand of LaunchGremlin, but should read as its own standalone mark. Do NOT reuse or reference the LaunchGremlin gremlin mascot/character in the Landora logo or icons — Landora's register is deliberately softer and more abstract than LaunchGremlin's "Sharp Mischief" voice.

## 3. Logo Style Direction
- **Type of mark:** wordmark-led, optionally paired with a small abstract symbol (not a mascot, not a literal object like a house/page icon cliché).
- **Symbol concept options** (pick one direction, don't blend):
  1. An abstract mark suggesting a "closed loop" or "arrival point" — implying a visitor's journey ending in a conversion (e.g., a line that resolves into a dot or a simple checkmark-adjacent shape, abstracted so it doesn't read as a literal checkmark).
  2. A minimal geometric monogram built from the letter "L" — clean, single-weight, no ornamentation.
  3. No symbol at all — a confident wordmark only, differentiated purely through custom letter spacing/weight.
- **What to avoid:** gremlins, cartoon characters, gradients, drop shadows, glossy/3D effects, stock "conversion funnel" or "arrow up" clichés, overly rounded "friendly SaaS" bubble letters.

## 4. Color Palette
- **Base — Cream:** #F5F1EA (or near-white/off-white background)
- **Base — Near-black (ink):** #1A1A18
- **Accent — Signal Green:** #1F6F4C (use sparingly — one accent only, not a rainbow)
- Logo should work as a single-color mark first (all ink, or all cream on a dark background) before adding the green accent. The green, if used, should sit on ONE element only (e.g., the dot in the symbol, or a single letterform detail) — never as a background fill.

## 5. Typography
- **Display/headline typeface:** Fraunces (confident serif — use for any wordmark treatment that leans editorial)
- **Body/UI typeface:** Inter Tight (clean sans — use if the wordmark should read as more product/software-like rather than editorial)
- **Utility/mono typeface:** IBM Plex Mono (for any small print, tagline treatment, or technical/code-like accents)
- For the primary logo, choose EITHER the Fraunces serif treatment (more editorial, premium-print feel) OR a custom sans treatment inspired by Inter Tight (more product/app feel) — do not mix both in one mark.

## 6. Icon Set Requirements
Generate the following variations from the finalized mark:
1. **Primary logo** — full wordmark (+ symbol if chosen), horizontal lockup, for light (cream) background
2. **Reversed logo** — same lockup, for dark (near-black) background
3. **Square app icon** — symbol only (or a monogram if no symbol was chosen), for use as a favicon/app icon at small sizes (needs to remain legible at 32x32px)
4. **Favicon set** — simplified single-color version of the square icon, works at 16x16px
5. **Social avatar** — square crop of the icon, safe margins for circular cropping (platforms like X/LinkedIn crop to a circle)

## 7. Voice/Feel Checklist (for the AI generating the visuals)
- Confident, not loud
- Minimal, not empty
- Premium, not corporate-stiff
- Product-like, not agency-mascot-like
- Should feel like it could sit on an Apple Store shelf, not a startup pitch deck

## 8. What NOT to Include
- No gremlin, no mascot, no character illustration of any kind
- No gradients, no 3D bevels, no drop shadows
- No stock icons (funnels, arrows, houses, browser windows)
- No more than one accent color
- No script/handwritten fonts
